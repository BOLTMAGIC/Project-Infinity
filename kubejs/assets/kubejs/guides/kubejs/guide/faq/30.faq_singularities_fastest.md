# **Q: How do I make Singularities the fastest?**
**Q: How do I make Singularities the fastest?**

Short answer: If you do not have an ME Infinity Cell, use Mekanism pumps + water into a Matter Condenser; if you do, use an Extended IO Port + Infinity Cell setup.

Setup 1 – No ME Infinity Cell (water into Matter Condenser):
* If you do not already have an ME Infinity Cell, the fastest way is to pump water into a Matter Condenser using Mekanism Electric Pumps.
* Place 4 Electric Pumps (each with 16 Speed and 16 Energy Upgrades) pumping water into an Oversized Interface using Mechanical Pipes.
* Connect an ME Glass Cable from the Oversized Interface to a Storage Bus on the side of a Matter Condenser:
  * Put a 1K ME Storage Component inside the Matter Condenser.
  * Set the Matter Condenser to make Matter Balls.
* Power this small AE2 subnet with:
  * An Energy Acceptor and a Superdense Energy Cell.
* Use a Reaction Chamber recipe that converts Matter Balls into Singularities using lava.
* Important:
  * Do not connect this to your main AE2 network, or it will flood your fluid cells with water.
  * You need Advanced Mechanical Pipes and Universal Cables; Basic is not enough.
  * This setup with 4 fully upgraded Electric Pumps uses about 96 388 FE/t.
  * For faster production, add more Electric Pumps; you do not need to upgrade the pipes or cables further.
  * Do not forget to enable Bypass Limit on the Flux Point feeding this subnet.

Setup 2 – With ME Infinity Cell:
* If you already have an ME Infinity Cell, use this setup:
  * Place an ME Extended IO Port with 5 Acceleration Cards.
  * Connect an ME Glass Cable from the Extended IO Port to a Storage Bus on top of a Matter Condenser:
    * Put a 64K ME Storage Component inside the Matter Condenser.
    * Set the Matter Condenser to make Singularities directly.
  * Power this small AE2 subnet with an Energy Acceptor and a Superdense Energy Cell.
* Important:
  * Do not connect this to your main AE2 network, or it will again flood your fluid cells with water.
  * This setup uses between 1 000 000 and 3 000 000 FE/t.
  * You must enable Bypass Limit on the Flux Point, or the power cap will throttle your singularity production.
