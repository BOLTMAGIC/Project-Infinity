# **Q: How do I automate the infinity 9 block in the entropy cradle?**
**Q: How do I automate the infinity 9 block in the entropy cradle?**

Short answer: follow a simple builder-based setup and adjust the timer if crafts are skipped.

* Take a look at this video for a full setup:
  [Link](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tP8O12OYQlQ)
* The setup is very easy to build and lets the Entropy Cradle craft Infinity 9 blocks at nearly max speed.
* If you notice that it sometimes does not craft all the blocks you requested:
  * Try changing the timer that triggers the Builder to 7 or 8 ticks.
  * This usually fixes timing issues where the cradle misses some crafts.
