# **Q: How do I get the most hp?**
**Q: How do I get the most hp?**

Short answer: combine cyclic hearts, heart gainer, heart/soul amulets with canisters, and the ring of odin.

* You can get a large amount of HP by:
  * Eating **40 Cyclic Hearts**.
  * Using **10 Heart Gainer** ability levels.
  * Wearing a **Heart or Soul Amulet** with **Heart Canisters** in it in your Curio slots.
  * Equipping a **Ring of Odin** in a Curio slot.
* Important:
  * Take the Heart/Soul Amulet and Heart Gainers off **before** trying to add Cyclic Hearts.
  * If you keep them on while eating Cyclic Hearts, the hearts will not apply correctly.
