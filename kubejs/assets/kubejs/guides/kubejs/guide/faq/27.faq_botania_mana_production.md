# **Q: How do I increase my mana production?**
**Q: How do I increase my mana production?**

Short answer: scale up early flowers, then move into mid-tier generators, and finally into mana collector + greenhouse setups.

* Early game:
  * Add more endoflames (and, if needed, more mana spreaders and mana pools).
  * Make sure your fuel supply (charcoal, etc.) keeps up with the extra flowers.


* Mid-tier flowers:
  * Move into middle-tier generating flowers like **Kekimurus** (cakes) or **Entropynniums** (TNT).
  * These give a big boost if you can automate their inputs.


* Mana collector and greenhouse:
  * A good medium‑ to long‑term goal is to build a **mana collector** and a **greenhouse**.
  * With a mana collector, Entropynniums become extremely strong if you can produce enough TNT.
  * The collector lets flower‑generated mana go directly into a spark network, which scales much better than spreaders.


* Late game / final setups:
  * The typical final mana generation setup is **Wither Aconites** in greenhouses.
  * This combo provides very high, scalable mana production once you reach that stage.
