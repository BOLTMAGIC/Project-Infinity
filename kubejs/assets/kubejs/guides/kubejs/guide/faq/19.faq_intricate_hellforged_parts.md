# **Q: How do I get intricate hellforged parts?**
**Q: How do I get intricate hellforged parts?**

Short answer: run the Pathway to the Endless Realm ritual, find the Foreman's Key, then open and farm the mine.

* Video guide:
  * [Link](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=D39UUisEFAA)

Ritual setup:
* Use a Ritual Diviner [ Dusk ] to select the "Pathway to the Endless Realm" ritual.
* You need 69 Ritual Stones for this ritual.
* Build the ritual and right‑click the Master Ritual Stone with a Weak Activation Crystal.
* Make sure you have at least 150 000 LP in your blood network before activating.

Finding the mine and the Foreman's Key:
* Explore the Endless Realm using Iron Keys.
* When you enter a room and chat says:
  * "You feel a spatial distortion in this room..."
  * That room has a special lock that looks different from the normal ones.
* Mark that room with a waypoint – this is the **mine entrance**.
* Keep exploring until you get **another** "spatial distortion" message in chat:
  * That second room also has a special lock.
  * Behind that lock is a small room with a chest that contains the **Foreman's Key**.
* Go back to your marked mine entrance and use the Foreman's Key on that special lock.

Farming Intricate Hellforged Parts:
* After opening the mine, explore it using **Miner's Keys** (not Iron Keys anymore).
* Intricate Hellforged Parts are found in chests inside this mine area.
* They are rare:
  * Example results: 19 Intricate Hellforged Parts from 64 Miner’s Keys (no extra luck).
  * With a Mystical Agriculture armor piece with a Luck 3 augment (permanent Luck 3),
    up to 82 Intricate Hellforged Parts from 64 Miner’s Keys is possible.
* Thanks to @Sohail160 for confirming that **luck works** on these chests.

Important warning:
* If you already explored the dungeon and used 100+ keys without watching chat:
  * You have likely missed the key "spatial distortion" messages.
  * In that case, make a new Pathway to the Endless Realm ritual and start again from a fresh dungeon.
