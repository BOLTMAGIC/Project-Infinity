# **Q: What's the best altar setup?**
**Q: What's the best altar setup?**

Short answer: different rune layouts are optimal for different goals (double compressed slate, Infinity 4, ethereal slate, and charging orbs).

* General notes:
  * All setups assume reinforced runes, a tier 5 altar, and crafting a stack at a time.
  * These layouts are optimized for piping blood in using fire‑attuned setups to generate blood.
  * The Blood Assembler added in 0.50 can do about 200 crafts per minute:
    * For Infinity 4 and Double Compressed Slates, the altars below are better.
    * For tier 2–5 slates, the Blood Assembler is faster.
